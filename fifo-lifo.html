📖 Comprehensive FIFO vs AVCO Reference Guide (IAS 2)

1. Core Inventory Accounting Equation

Beginning Inventory + Purchases = Cost of Goods Available for Sale (COGAS)
COGAS − Ending Inventory = Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
Sales Revenue − COGS = Gross Profit

2. The 4 Combinations at a Glance (IAS 2 Permitted)

Method System Ending Inventory Allocation COGS Allocation
FIFO Periodic From LATEST purchases in period backwards From OLDEST purchases forwards
FIFO Perpetual Continuous queue remaining on hand Continuous queue oldest depleted first (Identical to Periodic!)
AVCO Periodic Ending units × Weighted Average Cost per unit Units sold × Weighted Average Cost per unit
AVCO Perpetual Ending units × Latest Moving Average rate Each sale costed at Current Moving Average rate

3. Inflationary Impact (Rising Prices)

When purchase costs rise over time:
• FIFO assigns older (cheaper) costs to COGS, leaving recent (higher) costs in Ending Inventory $\to$ Lower COGS, Higher Gross Profit, Higher Tax.
• AVCO calculates a weighted average rate across all layers $\to$ Smooths out cost spikes, produces balanced COGS and stable profit margins.

4. IFRS & IAS 2 Regulatory Compliance

• IFRS (IAS 2) Standards: Only FIFO and Weighted Average Cost (AVCO) are permitted. LIFO is strictly forbidden under IAS 2 because it understates inventory carrying amounts on the Statement of Financial Position with outdated historical costs.
• Both FIFO and AVCO ensure inventories are reported at the lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV).

5. Double-Entry Journal Entries

• Perpetual System:
  1. On Purchase: Dr Inventory, Cr Accounts Payable / Cash
  2. On Sale: Dr Accounts Receivable, Cr Sales Revenue
  3. Cost of Sale: Dr Cost of Goods Sold, Cr Inventory (Recorded simultaneously with every sale)
• Periodic System:
  1. On Purchase: Dr Purchases, Cr Accounts Payable / Cash
  2. On Sale: Dr Accounts Receivable, Cr Sales Revenue (No entry made to Inventory or COGS at point of sale)
  3. Year-End Count: Dr Ending Inventory (SOFP), Dr Cost of Goods Sold (SPL), Cr Opening Inventory, Cr Purchases

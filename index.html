ACCA Financial Accounting (FFA / FA) Quick Reference

1. DEAD CLIC Rules

Debit increases: Expenses, Assets, Drawings (Decreases are Credited).
Credit increases: Liabilities, Income, Capital (Decreases are Debited).

2. The Extended Accounting Equations

• Fundamental Equation: Assets = Capital + Liabilities
• Net Assets Equation: Net Assets (Assets − Liabilities) = Capital
• Dynamic Business Equation: Closing Net Assets = Opening Net Assets + Capital Introduced + Profit for the Year − Drawings

3. Trial Balance Errors

• Errors not affecting Trial Balance (TB still balances): Omission, Commission, Principle, Compensating, Original Entry, Complete Reversal.
• Errors affecting Trial Balance (Requires Suspense Account): Single entry, Casting error, Extraction error, Transposition on one side.

4. IAS 2 Inventory Valuation

Inventory must be valued at the lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV) on an item-by-item basis. NRV = Expected Selling Price − Completion Costs − Selling Expenses.

5. Chapter 8: Property, Plant & Equipment (IAS 16)

• Capital vs Revenue: Capital expenditure enhances/creates PPE (site prep, installation, carriage in, dismantling provision). Revenue expenditure maintains performance (servicing, repairs, operator training, insurance).
• Straight-Line Depreciation: (Cost − Residual Value) / Useful Life.
• Reducing Balance Depreciation: Carrying Amount × Rate %.
• Revaluation Model: Upward revaluation recognized in OCI / Revaluation Surplus. Annual reserves transfer permitted: Dr Revaluation Surplus, Cr Retained Earnings.
• Asset Disposals: Profit/Loss = Disposal Proceeds / Trade-in − Carrying Amount.

6. Accruals & Prepayments

• Prepayments: Paid in advance $\to$ Current Asset on SOFP, deducted from cash paid in SPL.
• Accruals: Consumed unbilled $\to$ Current Liability on SOFP, added to cash paid in SPL.
• Working Formula: SPL Charge = Cash Paid + Opening Prepayment − Closing Prepayment − Opening Accrual + Closing Accrual.

7. Irrecoverable Debts & Allowances (IFRS 9)

• Write-Off: Dr Irrecoverable Debts Expense, Cr Trade Receivables (removes bad debtor).
• Allowance Calculation: Apply % to Adjusted Receivables (after write-offs).
• Allowance Adjustment: Increase is an expense in SPL; Decrease is a credit/income in SPL.
• SOFP Presentation: Trade Receivables minus Closing Allowance = Net Receivables.
